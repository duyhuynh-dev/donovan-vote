HOW TO READ THIS

Patterns, not predictions.

Election colors show each state’s statewide presidential winner. The 2024 vote shares come from the supplied project CSV. For 2008–2020, the map shows statewide winners only. Maine and Nebraska are colored by their statewide result, even in years when electoral votes split by district.

Google Trends scores are normalized from 0–100 for the selected term, location, and date window. Comparing state scores can reveal a geographic pattern, but it cannot tell us how a person voted or why they searched.
